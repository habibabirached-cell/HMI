Wisconsin 1 GW: CAPEX and gas price rescale project LCOE.

This is Charu’s formula for the Wisconsin 1 GW case only. The divisors are the same prices the screen starts on: solar 1,600, wind 2,000, 7HA 2,500, LM2500 3,000, battery 238, gas 3.50. The 7HA and the LM2500 each keep their own year-1 energy. Texas 1 GW uses different divisors and a thermal rule. The configurator applies this formula. It has a gas price on each turbine: the 7HA price scales 7HA fuel, and the LM2500 price scales LM2500 fuel. When those two prices match, that is the single fuel factor below.

1 · FactorsYour price ÷ the Wisconsin 1 GW study price.
2 · Technology LCOEScaled CAPEX, plus O&M. One gas factor scales 7HA fuel and LM2500 fuel.
3 · BlendWeight each LCOE by that plant’s own year-1 energy.
4 · ProjectMultiply the original project LCOE by blend-new ÷ blend-study.

Charu’s Wisconsin 1 GW formulas

The prices below are the study divisors and the screen’s starting prices. A price left on its divisor leaves that piece of LCOE unchanged.

Study divisors

Solar0 = 1,600$/kW
Wind0 = 2,000$/kW
HDGT0 = 2,500$/kW · 7HA
Aero0 = 3,000$/kW · LM2500
BESS0 = 238$/kWh
Gas0 = 3.50$/MMBtu · both turbines

Scaling factors

solaradj = solar CAPEXuser1,600    windadj = wind CAPEXuser2,000    hdgtadj = 7HA CAPEXuser2,500

aeroadj = LM2500 CAPEXuser3,000    bessadj = battery CAPEXuser238    fueladj = gas priceuser3.50

Each technology’s LCOE

Solar LCOEuser = (Solar CAPEX LCOE0 × solaradj) + Solar OM LCOE

Wind LCOEuser = (Wind CAPEX LCOE0 × windadj) + Wind OM LCOE

BESS LCOEuser = (BESS CAPEX LCOE0 × bessadj) + BESS OM LCOE

7HA LCOEuser = (7HA CAPEX LCOE0 × hdgtadj) + (7HA Fuel LCOE0 × fueladj) + 7HA OM LCOE

LM2500 LCOEuser = (LM2500 CAPEX LCOE0 × aeroadj) + (LM2500 Fuel LCOE0 × fueladj) + LM2500 OM LCOE

Blend, then scale the project

adjustment = BlenduserBlend0

Project LCOEuser = Project LCOE0 × adjustment

Project LCOEuser is the value this formula would show for each simulation after the prices move. On the sheet that column is spelled “Project LOCE”.
