Installed CAPEX and gas price rescale project LCOE.

Martin’s 200 MW cases were priced at one set of study costs. When you move the installed-CAPEX or gas-price wheels and open Explore Data, only the capital (and fuel) piece of each technology’s LCOE moves. Operations cost stays as studied. The project LCOE then shifts by the same ratio as the energy-weighted blend.

1 · FactorsYour wheel ÷ the study price.
2 · Technology LCOEScaled CAPEX, plus O&M. Fuel scales for the LM2500.
3 · BlendWeight each LCOE by that case’s year-1 energy.
4 · ProjectMultiply the original project LCOE by blend-new ÷ blend-study.

What Explore Data shows. The number on each simulation is Project LCOE at the study prices × (your blend ÷ the study blend). The least-cost case is chosen from those updated values. The simple-cycle heavy-duty wheel does not enter the 200 MW math. Gas price is the aeroderivative (LM2500) wheel. Texas 1 GW uses a different formula. Wisconsin 1 GW uses its own formula: these same divisors, with the 7HA included.

Charu’s formulas

The prices below are fixed for the whole 200 MW study. They do not change from row to row. A wheel at one of these prices leaves that piece of LCOE unchanged.

Defaults

Solar CAPEX0 = 1,600$/kW
Wind CAPEX0 = 2,000$/kW
Aero CAPEX0 = 3,000$/kW · LM2500
BESS CAPEX0 = 238$/kWh
Gas0 = 3.50$/MMBtu
HDGT CAPEX0 = 2,500$/kW · defined, not used on 200 MW

Scaling factors

solaradj = solar CAPEXuser1,600    windadj = wind CAPEXuser2,000    aeroadj = LM2500 CAPEXuser3,000

bessadj = battery CAPEXuser238    fueladj = gas priceuser3.50    hdgtadj = HDGT CAPEXuser2,500 hdgtadj is not used in the 200 MW case.

Each technology’s LCOE

Solar LCOEuser = (Solar CAPEX LCOE0 × solaradj) + Solar OM LCOE

Wind LCOEuser = (Wind CAPEX LCOE0 × windadj) + Wind OM LCOE

BESS LCOEuser = (BESS CAPEX LCOE0 × bessadj) + BESS OM LCOE

LM2500 LCOEuser = (LM2500 CAPEX LCOE0 × aeroadj) + (LM2500 Fuel LCOE0 × fueladj) + LM2500 OM LCOE

Project LCOEuser = Project LCOE0 × adjustment
